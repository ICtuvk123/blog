Background

  • Recent research generalizes traditional cross-modal alignment but requires all modalities to be present -> challenging to utilize prevalent datasets with missing modalities

main contribution

  • We introduce Calibrated Multimodal Representation Learning (CalMRL) to address the overlooked missing modalities dilemma
  • we propose using a generative model to impute oracle modalities
  • We conduct extensive experiments to demonstrate the superiority of CalMRL and provide strong empirical evidence supporting our design rationale. Comprehensive empirical studies and discussions are also provided.

Experiment

  • RQ1: Does CalMRL outperform other multimodal representation methods under the missing-modality setting?
  • RQ2: What is the contribution of CalMRL to different missing modalities? Does it satisfy the theoretical insight to calibrate for a better alignment with empirical results?
  • RQ3: How is the training of CalMRL stable? Can it maintain the distribution of multimodal representations?

Datasets We first adopt the VAST-150K with complete modalities for warming up parameters, following [14, 15]. Then we train the model on datasets with missing modality. Specifically, we select two datasets: MSRVTT [44] for vision-text pairs and AudioCaps [28] for audio-text pairs, covering two mainstream types of multimodal datasets (i.e., V↔T, A↔T)
