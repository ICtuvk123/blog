Method

overview

!Pasted image 20260226151754.png $$ \begin{equation} \ln p(x_J \mid \theta, J) \ge \mathbb{E}_{Z \sim q(Z \mid \phi, x_J)} \Big[ \ln p(x_J \mid \eta, Z, J) + \ln p(Z \mid \psi) - \ln q(Z \mid \phi, x_J) \Big]. \tag{1} \end{equation} $$ where $\theta = \{\eta, \psi, \phi\}$ are the parameters of the model. We propose to model these parameters using neural networks, namely: $$ \phi(x_J) = \mathrm{NN}_{\mathrm{enc}}(x_J), \quad \eta(Z, J) = \mathrm{NN}_{\mathrm{dec}}(Z, J), $$ and $\psi$ are weights of a parameterization of the prior. Since our assumption is that the model must be exchangeable, we propose to parameterize the distributions in a way that: (i) $Z$ is permutation-invariant, namely, we aim for defining variational posteriors as Gaussian distributions with permutation-invariant neural networks $\mathrm{NN}_{\mathrm{enc}}$; (ii) the conditional likelihood is defined as $$ p(x_J \mid \eta(Z, J)) = \prod_{i \in J} p(x_i \mid \eta_i(Z, J)), $$ hence, we must ensure that: $$ \mathbf{P}\eta(Z, \pi(J)) = \mathrm{NN}(Z, \pi(J)). $$
