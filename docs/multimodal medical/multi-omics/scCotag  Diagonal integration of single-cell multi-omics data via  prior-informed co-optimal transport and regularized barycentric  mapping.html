Related work

diagonal integration

method

  1. relies on biological prior knowledge to link features across omic layers, thereby imputing one modality from the other
  2. some methods directly infer cell correspondences from the intra-modality geometric structure, under the assumption that a shared latent manifold structure is preserved between modalities
  3. Deep learning-based methods extend these ideas by learning low-dimensional cell representations and projecting them into a modality-shared latent space for cell alignment

problem

  1. priors derived from external paired datasets or from genome coordinates are inherently context-dependent and noisy
  2. In practice, this assumption is frequently violated because real-world datasets often contain modality-unique cell types and imbalanced compositions
  3. suffer from noisy priors and restrictive assumption that all cells are alignable, providing limited mechanisms to prevent over-alignment of modality-specific populations.
