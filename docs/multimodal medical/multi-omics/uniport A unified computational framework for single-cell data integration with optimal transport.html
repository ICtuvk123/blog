gene score

Given that our integration of scATAC is based on gene activity score, we also tested uniPort’s performance when gene activity score is calculated in different approaches. We employed two methods to form the gene activity score introduced by Signac58 and MAESTRO37, respectively. Signac defines gene activity score for scATAC as the read count in the gene body and promoter region. MAESTRO calculates gene activity score as a weighted sum of nearby cis-regulatory elements (REs), where the weight is an exponentially decreasing function of distances of REs and target genes. The integration result showed that uniPort achieved better performance on the gene activity score by MAESTRO with all evaluated scores higher than that by Signac (Supplementary Fig. 13), which demonstrated the importance of modeling the gene activity score.
