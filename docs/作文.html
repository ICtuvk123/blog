The Influence of Short Videos on Young People

短视频平台正在深刻影响年轻人的学习、娱乐和社交方式。有人认为短视频让信息获取更加便捷，也有人担心它会削弱年轻人的专注力和独立思考能力。请你围绕这一现象，分析其原因、影响，并提出你的看法。

In today's world, ubiquitous access to short videos has transformed the way we study , entertain and socialize. This trend has sparked heated debates among the public.

Some people consider it as a positive signal. Coincident with the rise of short-video platforms has been the growing ease of acquiring information. , which enables us to pick out the personalized content that was previously unimaginable. Others, however , emphasize its negative effects. This kind of media has posed a growing menace to our ability to focus and think independently. Algorithms prioritizing engagement over depth, people at a young age can hardly distinguish the thoughtful videos from the shallow ones.

Notwithstanding the disadvantages, we can still make changes to overcome these hazards. Subscribing to more high quality channels or choosing videos that are consistent with our interest can be of great help. Only when we use short videos as a tool for learning rather than a trap for attention can they truly benefit our future life.
